# Scientific English for Researchers

Open, interactive book for doctoral students and researchers, especially Spanish speakers: the architecture of research articles, clear sentences, hedging and stance, common errors and false friends, peer review and academic correspondence, and presenting and networking in English.

**Read it at:** <https://pablosancheznunez.com/scientific-english/> (also as PDF and EPUB).

- Browser labs: readability checker, hedge and booster highlighter, Spanglish detector. Activities with instant feedback, a reviewer scenario, self-assessment (also as Moodle XML) and assignments.
- Works offline, light and dark mode, WCAG 2.1 AA.

## Citation

Sánchez-Núñez, P. (2026). *Scientific English for Researchers: Writing, reviewing and presenting research in English*. Universidad Carlos III de Madrid. Zenodo. <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24538351>
